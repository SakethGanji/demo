# D3 · Model Doctor 2 and What's missing

**What it does.** The run page's Doctor tab (`models?run=…&tab=Doctor`) now opens with two cards:

- **What's missing** ranks the information the table lacks by measured potential gain. Each gap is turned into a
  concrete data request ("collect", "join", "redefine the label", "fix the source").
- **How to improve** is one ranked list of levers, each with a measured Δ on the same holdout, or the typed experiment
  that measures it.

Below them is the diagnosis, with new rules:

- **Leakage** fires on dominance plus a Data Doctor hint, and comes with a one-click **ablation**.
- **Cost trade-off** replaces weak class and imbalance on cost runs.
- **At the data's limit** fires when tuning cannot help.

A new **Holdout Errors** sub-tab shows where the model went wrong. Everything is deterministic: every number comes
from code, and every rule is on screen.

## Backend

### `backend/app/vela/missing.py` (new): the measurements, one job, each part fails alone

| Part | How |
|---|---|
| **Quick model** | LightGBM (200 trees, lr 0.05, 31 leaves, seed 0) on the run's features (text and ID columns left out), ≤ 30k training rows. Its holdout score is shown. |
| **Errors** | Holdout rows whose served decision is wrong (regression: the top 20 % absolute error), ranked by confidence. The reasons are the quick model's **TreeSHAP** (`pred_contrib`). The served model's exact Kernel SHAP is one click away (Explain → Features & SHAP). Also: cleanlab flags on the holdout p̂, the share of the errors flagged vs the share of the correct rows, and errors by time (5 bins, χ²) when a date exists. |
| **Concentration** | Error rate by bin of every feature (quintiles of numbers, levels of categories, missing as its own bin; bins fit on the whole column). Lift, excess errors, one-sided binomial z, BH q. Kept when lift ≥ 1.25 and q ≤ 0.05; ranked by excess errors. Missingness of each feature inside the error rows, and **inside each segment's errors vs outside the segment**. |
| **Twins** | The 15 nearest *training* rows of every row in importance-weighted percentile space (categories one-hot; a full-range gap and a different category both cost the feature's weight). U = 1 − Σ K_c(K_c−1)/(k(k−1)) is unbiased for 1 − Σ η_c², so R_NN = mean U is the nearest-neighbour error. **Cover–Hart:** R* ∈ [(K−1)/K·(1 − √(1 − K/(K−1)·R_NN)), R_NN]. Bootstrap CIs; the model's error is an upper bound too. Also: the share of errors on mixed rows (U ≥ 0.3), where the look-alikes concentrate, and D1's exact conflicts. |
| **Thin features** | ≤ 12 values, ≥ 5 % of the permutation importance, and ≥ 80 % of the outcome's variance left inside the levels. |
| **Symmetric confusion** | Multiclass pairs with both directions ≥ 3 errors and min/max ≥ 0.4. Gain: their errors ÷ n, the accuracy upper bound if they were merged. |
| **Quick learning curve** | A **regularised, early-stopped** LightGBM (15 leaves, ≥ 40 rows a leaf, subsampling, lr 0.03, early stop on 15 % of its own rows) on nested 1/8, 1/4, 1/2 and all training rows. Next doubling = last gain × the mean decay of successive gains, with a paired bootstrap CI. **Flat:** < ε = 0.005 and upper bound < 2ε. *The first version used the fixed 200-tree model: on Taiwan its 1/8 point was 0.025 AUC low, which inflated the gain to +0.010 per doubling.* |
| **Oracle (simulation)** | The quick model refit with one extra column: the label with 10 % noise inside one segment and missing elsewhere. Δ holdout with a paired bootstrap, run for the top 3 concentration segments and the mixed-neighbourhood ("twin") rows. Labelled **simulation** and "up to" everywhere. |
| **Clean labels** | cleanlab on the quick model's 3-fold out-of-fold p̂ over the training rows. The quick model is refit without the flagged rows; Δ is on the untouched holdout. |
| **Leak signals** | Permutation-importance share of the top feature and its ratio to the next. D1 `single_auc` for the top 3. D1 `check_leakage` suspects with their reasons. |
| **Cost** | Decline rate, catch rate, cost per 1,000 at the served t and at the 3:1 / 5:1 / 10:1 Bayes thresholds t = 1/(1+r). The cost per 1,000 is computed under the run's costs and under r with c_FP = 1. |

### `backend/app/vela/experiments.py` (new): typed experiments

- **Routes:** `POST /api/runs/{id}/experiments {kind: ablate, columns} | {kind: budget} | {kind: preset}`, and `GET …/experiments`.
- **Lineage:** the child run is the run's own body with one change, with `parent_run_id`, `source: doctor-experiment`
  and `patch.experiment`.
- **Δ:** child − parent on the **shared holdout rows**, paired bootstrap (B = 1,000), "better is positive". The
  metric is AUC; for a cost run, cost / 1,000 at each run's served t; accuracy for multiclass; RMSE for regression.
- **Refusals (400):** a free-form kind, unknown columns, an empty column list, or ablating every feature. An unknown
  run → 404.
- **For D4:** typed hypotheses can go through this route.

### `backend/app/vela/doctor.py`: the decision procedure

- **Leakage**
  - It is critical when both hold:
    - one feature has **≥ 50 % of the permutation importance and ≥ 3× the next**;
    - the Data Doctor or Smart Intake names it **by its name or values**, and nobody answered it is known at decision time.
  - It is also critical when a feature **alone has AUC ≥ 0.97**.
  - An identifier used as a feature is major (Vela's check).
  - Strength alone is **not** a hint. The first version counted D1's "AUC ≥ 0.90" reason as a hint, and the F17 shift
    scenario's legitimate rule column (73 % of importance, AUC 0.949) became a leak.
  - The prescription is the ablation experiment. Once it has run, the card shows **with / without / Δ CI** and the
    prescription becomes "Open the run without it".
- **Cost trade-off:** major on every binary cost run. Weak class and imbalance become n/a there.
- **At the data's limit:** minor, when all four hold:
  - a flat curve (F12 saturated, otherwise the quick curve);
  - the ladder's single GBM within the interval of the best (or the best itself);
  - no overfit finding;
  - no fixable weak slice.

  It is clear when a leak is in. The prescription is the best measured lever.
- **Precedence re-tuned:** leakage › shift › label noise › overfit › **cost trade-off** › data-limited › underfit ›
  latency › weak slice › budget › **ceiling** › imbalance › weak class. The rule is still severity first, then
  precedence.
- **`missing_info` (schema `missing/1`)**
  - The gaps are ranked: a must-do first, then measured gains > 0, then unmeasured, then measured ≤ 0.
  - Also: the irreducible-error interval, the rules, and a **caveat while a leak is in** ("read them again on the
    ablation run").
  - A typed **`domain_gaps`** slot for D4: `{status: pending, source: llm, label: "Suggestions to investigate…", items: []}`.
- **`improve`:** levers for drop leak (the ablation Δ, a must-do), more rows, clean labels, new features (the newest
  Feature Lab job on the dataset or its parent; its Δ is on the AUC scale only), more budget and a stronger preset
  (experiments).
- **`errors_view`:** the data for the sub-tab.

### Ladder fix (D2's limit)

- `engine/ladder.py` scores binary cost runs by **expected cost per 1,000**, every rung deciding by the Bayes rule
  p > t*, lower is better (`metric: expected_cost`, `cost: {c_fp, c_fn, t}`).
- The sentence reads "… costs X more per 1,000 decisions than …".
- Crafted cost run: stack 472 vs baseline 808 per 1,000; the recommended rung is the single LightGBM.

## UI

- **Doctor tab, in this order:**
  - a one-line diagnosis;
  - **What's Missing:** the irreducible-error line, ranked gaps with finding → request and gain (CI, "simulation"
    pill), the D4 suggestion slot, and "How it is measured". Its one primary action is **Copy The Data Requests**.
  - **How To Improve:** ranked levers with measured Δ and how each was measured. The single primary action is the top
    lever's.
  - the primary card, with **leak detail** (suspects, with / without / Δ), the **cost table** (served, 3:1, 5:1, 10:1)
    or the **ceiling conditions** (✓ / ✕);
  - then the existing ranked factors, offers and every check.
- **Holdout Errors** (a new Doctor sub-tab; "Errors" stays Explain's):
  - the error count;
  - cleanlab overlap;
  - the most confident errors with reason chips and **SHAP →** (opens Explain → Features & SHAP for that row);
  - errors by slice, with missingness inside each slice's errors;
  - errors over time.
- **New action kinds:** `experiment` (starts the child; a note links to it), `open_run`, `open_view` (Threshold) and
  `link`. Feature Lab opens itself on `build?…&lab=1`.

### Hidden-feature test: Taiwan without PAY_0 (`run_8a4f31f2`)

Nothing tells the Doctor that PAY_0 was removed. Compared with the run that has PAY_0:

| | with PAY_0 | without PAY_0 |
|---|---|---|
| Holdout AUC | 0.778 | 0.753 |
| Headline | At the data's limit | At the data's limit |
| Model error | 0.181 | 0.196 |
| Irreducible error [lower, R_NN] | [0.158, 0.267] | **[0.177, 0.292]** |

| Twins oracle | +0.103 [+0.091, +0.115] | **+0.133 [+0.121, +0.146]** |

What's missing grows exactly where the information was taken: more look-alikes, a larger value of separating them,

a column it never saw. It points at the gap (the most recent repayment status, and a history finer than these codes)
through the twins and thin-feature requests. The concentration segments rank PAY_AMT bins above PAY_2 in both runs,
because the oracle gain scales with segment size.

  - The hidden-feature assertion should be relative (twins oracle and irreducible error up vs the full table), not
    "names the column".
- **Known limits:**
  - **Oracle gains scale with segment size.** Big quintile bins (Taiwan's PAY_AMT ≤ 1,000) rank high. A per-row
    normalisation (gain per 1,000 rows) may rank better.
  - **R_NN depends on the importance weights** and is biased up near borders of η. The Cover–Hart interval is
    reported, never a single number as truth.
  - **Quick-model Δs are AUC (binary) or accuracy (multiclass)**, not the run's cost metric.
  - **Text-only tables** get no twins, curve or oracle.
  - **"Clean labels" is measured with the quick model.** A full-ensemble retrain on cleaned rows needs a dataset
    version with the same holdout (D1's apply). It is not wired as an experiment yet.
  - **The bank ablation stays on `duration` until someone answers the decision-time question.** Then the leak check
    goes clear, as designed.
