# G6 · Time to event (survival)

**What it does.** A duration column T and a 0/1 event δ (1 = the event happened, 0 = censored) become two time-to-event models on the same rows: **Cox PH** and a **random survival forest**, both from scikit-survival 0.28. AutoGluon has no survival models, so this is its own small engine path, like anomaly. The holdout checks are:
- Harrell's C with a bootstrap CI;
- cumulative/dynamic AUC(τ) and Brier(τ) at the horizons;
- **calibration against Kaplan–Meier by risk quartile**;
- Cox hazard ratios with Wald CIs.

The result registers as a version with Try It (a survival curve per applicant), /score, batch scoring with PSI drift, and a model-card section.

Where it lives (no new navigation):
- **Build:** the suggestion "Time to churned (survival)", which opens `#/survival?dataset=&duration=&event=` (lights up Build).
- **Models:** the run page `#/survival?run=`, listed in Experiments as kind `survival`.
- **Registry:** a version with a **Survival** tab, plus Try It, Batch & Drift and Model Card.

## Backend
- **`survival/core.py`: a new run kind, `survival`.**
  - It runs in the run worker via `run_other`, so it has the same events, SSE and MLflow logging as other runs (engine tag `scikit-survival`).
  - It uses the F4 split, read with `load_frames`.
  - **Design:** numbers are median-imputed and standardised; categories are one-hot against a reference level. Categories with more than 30 levels are left out, and the page says so.
  - **Cox:** `CoxPHSurvivalAnalysis` with Breslow ties and a tiny ridge (α = 0.001). Standard errors come from the observed information of the partial likelihood (my own ~20 lines). HR is reported per unit of the original input and per SD.
  - **Forest:** 200 trees, min leaf 15, √d features per split.
  - **Which model serves:** the one with the lower **integrated Brier score** on a validation slice: the split's tuning slice, else training fold 0, which is held back from both fits. The holdout is never used to choose.
    - I first chose by validation C. On that rule the forest won by 0.002, but its curves are shrunk toward the average: max |Ŝ − KM| was 0.086 for the forest against 0.047 for Cox. IBS sees both ranking and calibration.
  - **Calibration rule:** at every horizon in every risk quartile, |mean Ŝ(τ) − Ŝ_KM(τ)| ≤ 0.05, or Ŝ falls inside the KM 95% log-log CI. Both models are reported; the served one decides the hero.
  - **Split default:** random, stratified by the event, or group when an entity exists. It is **not out-of-time**: the date is when follow-up starts, so the newest rows are mostly censored and could not check the later horizons. The page says why.
  - **Horizons:** taken from the unit in the name (months → 12, 24, 36; days, weeks and years likewise) and kept below the 90th percentile of T. Otherwise they are the quartiles of T.
- **Serving** (`score_rows`, `try_row`, `score_batch`):
  - each row gets its risk score, risk group (cut at the holdout quartiles), Ŝ(τ), P(event by τ) and median time;
  - Try It adds the full curve, the average subject's curve and, when Cox serves, the factors exp(β_j(z_j − z̄_j)). Their product is the hazard against the average subject; smoke checks this to 1e-6.
  - A batch adds the columns `risk_score`, `risk_group`, `S_12`, `S_24`, `S_36` and `median_time`, plus PSI of the inputs and of r̂ against the run's holdout scores.
- **`survival/api.py`:**
  - `GET /api/survival/detect`;
  - `POST /api/survival/runs`, which works with `{dataset_id}` alone;
  - `GET /api/survival/runs/{id}`;
  - `card_section`.
- **Registry:** `build_other` gains a survival branch (latency, holdout C and AUC(τ), the duration/event/horizons on the version). There are Try It and /score branches, and `score_flow_batch` gains a branch. `"survival"` is added to every non-tabular kind guard (Doctor, monitoring, credit report, shift, slices and others refuse it cleanly).
- **Model card:** a "Time to event (survival)" section in JSON, MD and the card view.
- **G0 detection (the duration role is broad, as G0 noted):**
  - The `survival` suggestion needs a duration role and a 0/1 event: an event role, or **the target itself** when it is a 0/1 flag with an event-like name, which is the churn case.
  - It scores **97 (top)** only for an *explicit* time-to-event name (`duration`, `time_to_…`, `months_to_…`, `survival`, `follow_up`, `_time`).
  - A tenure-like name scores **45**, with the caveat that it may be time so far at a snapshot, not time to the event. The Setup page repeats this warning.
  - account-churn (`tenure_months` + a Yes/No `churned`) gets no survival offer. That is correct, because its tenure is not time to churn; smoke asserts its top suggestion is still binary.
  - No new role was needed.
- **Sample `samples_g6.py` → `account-attrition`** (no existing sample touched):
  - 12,000 accounts with Weibull proportional hazards (k = 1.15, λ = 64);
  - one step effect on age that a linear Cox term can only approximate;
  - administrative censoring at 2025-12-31 plus 4%/year independent censoring;
  - 28.7% events;
  - the true η is kept in the sidecar: **true C = 0.778**.
- **Dependency:** `scikit-survival==0.28.0` (and ecos, numexpr, osqp) in `requirements.txt` and `requirements.lock`. It installed cleanly against scikit-learn 1.9.1.

## Numbers (smoke `surv`, zero-config: only `{dataset_id}` sent)

The split is random, stratified: 7,680 fit rows, 1,920 validation rows (fold 0) and 2,400 holdout rows (688 events, 1,712 censored). The fits take ~1 s (Cox 0.13 s, forest 1.0 s); the whole run ~9 s.

| model | holdout C [95% CI] | AUC(12) · AUC(24) · AUC(36) | max \|Ŝ − KM\| | validation C · IBS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cox PH (serves)** | **0.7652 [0.746, 0.786]** | 0.811 · 0.795 · 0.789 | **0.047** (9/12 in the KM CI) ✓ | 0.7594 · 0.1256 |
| random survival forest | 0.7581 [0.740, 0.777] | 0.803 · 0.789 · 0.782 | 0.086 (4/12) ✕ | 0.7616 · 0.1269 |

- **Acceptance: met.**
  - C = 0.765 > 0.7 (the lower CI bound is 0.746).
  - The served model's curves are calibrated against Kaplan–Meier in all 4 risk groups at 12, 24 and 36 months (max gap 0.047). The worst cell is the high group at 12 months: 0.645 predicted against 0.598 KM.
- **Hazard ratios recover the truth:**

  | term | estimate | truth |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | rate_vs_market_pp | 0.394 | exp(−0.9) = 0.41 |
  | num_products | 0.602 | 0.61 |
  | has_salary_mandate | 0.416 | 0.41 |
  | complaints_12m | 1.707 | 1.82 |

  The regions have no true effect, and every region CI covers 1.
- **The registered version** is 17.5 MB, with single-row p50 ≈ 6 ms and batch ≈ 36–39 µs/row.

**Smoke.** `SMOKE_ONLY=surv,intake,core make smoke` passes **38/38 in 105 s**:
- surv: 3 steps;
- intake: 15, including the new `account-attrition` truth row, 13/13 with top `survival`; the 11 other samples are unchanged at 100% and keep their top suggestions;
- core: 20.

## Limits and open problems
- **The binary suggestion on the same table keeps `duration_months` as an input.** It is post-outcome for a yes/no churn model. A leak flag for a strong duration when the target is the event belongs to G11's cohesion pass.
- **Events must be 0/1.** A Yes/No event column is not mapped. Only right censoring is handled: no left truncation (delayed entry), no competing risks (e.g. prepay against default) and no time-varying covariates.
- **The proportional-hazards assumption is stated, not tested.** There is no Schoenfeld-residual check. The forest is the fallback when PH fails, chosen by validation IBS.
- **The C-index CI** is a row bootstrap (200 draws); Uno's IPCW C is not reported.
- **When the forest serves,** Try It has no per-input factors (only the curve).
- **Not wired:** monitoring, credit report, fair lending, recourse, the Model Doctor and Vela. All refuse survival versions with a clear 400. Vela (G10) can call `POST /api/survival/runs`.
- **Fixed along the way:** curves before the first event time were clipped to Ŝ(t₁) instead of 1.
