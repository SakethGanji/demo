## What changed

**The job (`engine/active_learning.py`, new; its own process `python -m app.engine.active_learning <run_dir>`, with an
flock so only one process works on a queue).**
- **Fit:** every fit is F12's reusable `worker.prepare_fit` / `fit_predictor` call on the labelled rows, then
  `_decide_threshold` and `holdout_metrics`. The fixed holdout of the run's F4/F6 split scores every fit. The tuning slice
  keeps its labels, and group splits keep their fold column.
- **Pool:**
  - `hide`: h of the training rows, stratified, with ≥ 5 seed labels per class, seeded per comparison seed.
  - `missing`: the dataset's training rows whose label cell is empty.

  Holdout rows are never in the pool; the state reports `holdout_in_pool: 0`, and the smoke checks it against the run's
  holdout rows.
- **Uncertainty:** margin, entropy or least-confident, from the round's refit.
- **Diversity:** among the β·b most uncertain rows (β = 10):
  - weighted k-means (weights u) into b clusters, taking the most uncertain row of each (default);
  - greedy k-center against the labelled set;
  - or none.

  It runs on the Autopilot audit's embedding (`audit._embed`: TF-IDF→SVD, standardised numbers, one-hot).
- **Arms:**
  - `queue` is the real one: people answer it.
  - `active/s` and `random/s` (s = 0..S−1) are the simulated comparison. Each seed's round-0 fit is shared by both of
    its arms, so budgets are equal. Seed 0's round 0 and its first active batch are the queue's.

  Work is ordered: the queue's round 0 first, then submitted queue rounds before comparison fits, and the comparison goes
  round by round. Default cost: 17 fits, about 50 s on tickets.
- **Summary for F17** (`summary` in GET and in the run payload's `active_learning.summary`, `schema: active_learning/1`):
  ```
  {metric, greater_is_better, budget, rounds, strategy, diversity, pool: {source, hide_share, n_pool, n_initial},
   labels_used: [n per queue round], queue: [{round, n_labels, score}],
   comparison: {simulated: true, labeller, seeds, rounds: [{round, n, n_random, active: {mean, sd, scores},
                random: {…}, gap: {mean, sd, ci, level, wins, ties, n_seeds, per_seed, units}}]},
   verdict: {label: active-better | no-clear-difference | random-better | pending | not-compared, reason, round, n, gap, ci, wins, n_seeds}}
  ```

**API (`engine/active_learning_api.py`, new router).**
- `POST /api/runs/{id}/active-learning`:
  - 400 for a non-classification run, too few seed labels, `budget × rounds` larger than the hidden rows, or
    `pool = missing` without empty labels;
  - 422 for bad enums or seeds;
  - 409 while a queue is working.
- `GET` returns the queue items merged with their answers.
- `POST …/answer` takes S7's answer shape (confirm / relabel / skip / null) plus a `reviewer` name. It refuses unknown
  classes.
- `…/simulate` fills unanswered items from the hidden labels as reviewer `simulated-oracle`.
- `…/submit` closes the round (open items count as skipped) and makes a **dataset version with lineage** through
  `DatasetStore.create_version`:
  - the run's data with the still-unlabelled pool rows' labels empty and the answers filled in;
  - the same split record, with the structured split copied for F6;
  - lineage with `changes` (`hide`, then `label` per row with its reviewer and round).

  Then it refits. A second submit returns 409.
- `…/cancel`.
- `admin/reset` refuses while a queue is working.

**UI (Explain → Labelling Queue, classification runs).**
- **Hero:** the verdict, d̄ with its CI and wins, active and random ± sd, and your queue's labels used and score per round.
- **Chart:** score against labels used. Your queue is shown, plus the simulated active and random means with every seed
  and ±1 sd whiskers.
- **The queue:** the Ground-Truth Inbox's style and CSS. m(x) is bound to the selected row: the model's guess and its
  runner-up, u, rank and why. The answer buttons are confirm / relabel (with a select) / skip. Buttons: "Fill from Hidden
  Labels (Simulated)" and "Submit N Labels & Retrain".
- **Formula panels:** u(x), the candidate and diversity rule with ū picked against ū of the pool, and d_s with its
  t-interval, each bound to the numbers.
- **Tables:** your rounds (added, by whom, agreement with the hidden labels, dataset version) and active vs random per
  round.
- **Start card:** guided defaults plus an Advanced fold-out (pool, h, b, rounds, uncertainty, diversity, β, seeds,
  compare).

## Open problems

- **The effect is within the noise.** One refit's accuracy moves by ±0.005 (in one demo the queue arm went 0.9335 →
  0.9240 → 0.9360 on almost the same labels as the active arm). That is as large as the active-vs-random gap, so 3 seeds
  cannot separate them. 10 seeds (Advanced) would narrow the interval by about √(10/3) × the t ratio, at about 3× the
  fits. The sample's 300 planted label flips are in the oracle too, and uncertainty sampling seeks out the ambiguous
  multi-topic tickets.
- **Not deterministic.** The active arm's picks change between processes, by float-level differences in p̂ feeding
  k-means. The random arm is deterministic.
- **Pool = missing:**
  - It has no oracle, so it has no comparison (verdict `not-compared`).
  - A separate file of unlabelled rows is not accepted; append them to the dataset with blank labels.
  - Pool = missing with a group / time split takes rows from the split's training folds as they are.
- **Scheduling:**
  - One queue works at a time across the workbench, and it is not queued with runs or F12 curves, so it competes with
    them for CPU.
  - Starting again replaces the queue, including its answers; the dataset versions stay.
- **For F14 and F16:** answers carry a reviewer name, but there is one answer per item, so multi-annotator answers are
  F14's job. The "targeted labelling" link from F16's weak slices is not wired in yet. The queue picks from the whole pool.
