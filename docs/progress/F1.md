## What changed

**Backend (`app/forecast/core.py`, `api.py`).**
- **Conformal recalibration** (`conformal_fit` / `conformal_apply`): split-conformal scaling about the median, CQR-style,
  one c_τ per quantile level. On every backtest point (the best model's own `backtest_predictions`, never the holdout)
  r = (y − m)/(q̂_τ − m); c_τ = the ⌈(n+1)τ⌉-th smallest r for τ > ½ and the ⌈(n+1)(1−τ)⌉-th for τ < ½; q̃_τ = m + c_τ(q̂_τ − m),
  c_τ ≥ 0, quantiles sorted per row. The mean and the median do not move, so MASE, sMAPE, RMSE are unchanged; WQL is
  recomputed. `conformal_scope='item'` fits c_τ per series with ≥ 60 backtest points (pooled elsewhere). Opt-in field
  `conformal`, **true by default for new runs**. Both versions are recorded: `forecast.csv` / `holdout_forecast.csv`
  (recalibrated) and `forecast_raw.csv` / `holdout_forecast_raw.csv`, `holdout.calibration` and
  `holdout.calibration_raw`, `metrics` and `metrics_raw`, the chart's `q` and `q_raw`. c_τ is saved in the predictor
  folder (`ensemble_conformal.json`), so the forecast from new history and registry batches (which copy the folder)
  apply it too; the model card gets an interval-calibration line.
- **num_val_windows auto** (the new default, `null`): AutoGluon's own recommendation (5 under 20 series, else 3; its
  ≥ 100-series value of 2 raised to 3), capped by the shortest series (n_min ≥ H(W + 2) + 1). The rule is in
  `data.num_val_windows_rule` and the resolved call's notes.
- **Ensembles** (`ensemble`: `greedy` · `quantile` · `both`, default `both`): `quantile` is AutoGluon's
  `LinearStackerEnsemble(weights_per='mq')`, a softmax weight per model and quantile fitted by pinball loss; `both`
  fits it beside the greedy WeightedEnsemble and AutoGluon keeps the better on validation. The per-quantile weights are
  in `ensembles[].weights_per_quantile`. `fit_kwargs.ensemble_hyperparameters` still wins.
- **Per-model coverage**: every model's own holdout forecast, coverage of the widest interval with a Wilson CI and
  its mean backtest coverage: `holdout.calibration.per_model`, `leaderboard[].coverage_test` / `coverage_val`.

**UI (`features/forecast`).** Setup: W defaults to auto (the rule shown), an ensemble choice, the conformal checkbox
with its formula; per-series scope in Advanced; the resolved call shows `ensemble_hyperparameters`. Run: the Interval
Calibration panel plots F̂(τ) before (dashed) and after, the table has AutoGluon vs recalibrated coverage with the
Wilson CI and w̄/ȳ before → after, a verdict for each, the formula q̃_τ = m + c_τ(q̂_τ − m) bound to the fitted c_τ with
every symbol defined, and the per-model coverage table; the fan chart toggles recalibrated / AutoGluon bands; the
hero shows coverage and WQL with AutoGluon's beside; the leaderboard has a coverage column; the raw CSV is linked.

**Smoke.** The forecast run no longer pins `num_val_windows: 2` (it uses the default); the leaderboard step checks
W ≥ 3 windows; the new step `forecast: conformal recalibration (F1)` asserts coverage ∈ [0.85, 0.95], MASE ≤ 1.02 ×
0.515, unchanged point metrics, both ensemble kinds with per-quantile weights summing to 1, per-model coverage for
every leaderboard model, the four CSVs, non-crossing calibrated quantiles with a narrower band, and the options'
defaults; the new-history and model-card steps check that the recalibration travels with the predictor.

**Fixes on the way.**
- AutoGluon 1.6.3 bug that blocked the quantile-aware ensemble: the array-based ensembles (LinearStacker, Median,
  Tabular) build their label array from every column of the ground-truth windows, so with known covariates it has
  1 + |x| columns and the fit fails ("size of tensor a (3) must match … b (8)"). `patch_array_ensembles()` passes them
  the target column only (applied in the fit worker before `fit`).
- The run page's hero showed the top of the leaderboard (sorted by holdout score) under the best model's name; with
  W = 5 DirectTabular (0.520) edges the ensemble (0.521) on the holdout, so the hero now reads the best model's row.

## Open problems

- The guarantee is marginal and assumes exchangeability between backtest windows and the future; the scores come from
  the same windows that fitted the ensemble weights (6 scalars fitted on 840 points, so the optimism is small, but it
  is not a clean split). A held-out calibration window would cost one more window of history.
- One c_τ per level for every horizon step: if the over- or under-dispersion grows with h, early steps stay slightly
  wide and late steps slightly narrow. Per-horizon factors would need more backtest points.
- Per-series scope needs ≥ 60 backtest points per series (W × H); with W = 1 or short horizons it falls back to pooled.
- num_val_windows = 5 costs time (18 s vs 14 s for W = 3 here) and moved MASE by +1.2%; W = 3 gave 0.515 and 89.3%.
- Runs from before F1 have no `calibration_raw`/`conformal`; the page shows them as before (single curve).
