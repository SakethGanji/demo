# P18: teams, principals and members API, `/me`, team on every list row (lane G)

Plan: `docs/plans/data-platform-final.md` §6.1, §9.1 row P18, §11 (cross-team sharing yes: P21 builds the shares on
this model), C §1.1–1.2, §2.1, §5.1–5.2. Base: `motion-components` @ 2808acd. Authorisation mode is `off` (P19 adds
`require()`), so no route here refuses on role.

## What was built

- **`catalog/teams_api.py` (new)**:
  - `GET /api/me` (`?by=` or the `X-Ensemble-Actor` header) returns:
    - the principal;
    - `auth`: `typed`;
    - the caller's teams with their roles;
    - `default_team`: the only active team, or null when the caller is in several;
    - `authz_mode` (`ENSEMBLE_AUTHZ`, `off` by default).

    Without a name, it answers 200 with `principal: null` and a hint.
  - **Teams.**
    - `GET /api/teams` (`all=`, `mine=`) lists teams with `n_members`, `n_owners`, `n_projects` and `my_role`.
    - `POST /api/teams {name, slug?, description?, by}` answers 201. The creator becomes owner. The slug defaults to
      the name with accents folded, lower case and dashed. A duplicate slug gives 409.
    - `GET /api/teams/{t}` takes an id or a slug and returns the team with its members and projects.
    - `PATCH /api/teams/{t} {name?, description?, policy?, by}`. The policy must be a JSON object of at most 20k. It
      is audited with before and after.
  - **Members**: `GET/POST /api/teams/{t}/members` and `PATCH/DELETE /api/teams/{t}/members/{principal}`.
    - The principal is given by id or name.
    - `POST` takes exactly one of `principal`, `name` or `email`, plus `role`.
    - Roles are viewer, member, maintainer, owner and risk_reviewer.
    - Errors:
      - an existing member: 409, pointing to PATCH;
      - the last owner cannot be demoted or removed: 409;
      - the `system` principal: 400;
      - an unknown email: 404;
      - an archived team: 409.
  - **Principals.**
    - `GET /api/principals?q=&kind=` serves as the member picker.
    - `GET /api/principals/{p}` returns the principal with its teams.
    - `PATCH /api/principals/{p} {email?, display_name?, by}`. A display name that folds to another identity key is
      refused with 409: the four-eyes key never changes.
  - **First sight (P01 hand-off).**
    - `ensure_principal()` creates a principal for any new name: `pr_` + sha256(identity_key)[:12], the same id rule
      as the P01 backfill.
    - A person also joins `tm_default` as member, as the backfill did for non-admins. This is audited as
      `principal_created`.
    - On later sights, `ensure_principal()` updates `last_seen_at` and re-syncs `is_admin` from `ENSEMBLE_ADMINS`.
    - It runs from:
      - the `see_actor` middleware, for any request with `X-Ensemble-Actor`, at most once per name per 5 minutes per
        process;
      - every route here that takes a name;
      - `POST /projects`.
  - **Team on every list row.**
    - Rows get `team_id` and `team: {id, slug, name}`.
    - `/api/projects` adds them in its handler.
    - The `team_rows` middleware adds them to `/api/datasets`, `/api/data/search`, `/api/runs` (light and full),
      `/api/registry` (versions, and models from their newest version), `/api/registry/versions` and
      `/api/registry/summary`.
    - Owners are resolved in one query per list: dataset/run `team_id`, else the team of its project, else `tm_default`.
    - Lists are not filtered (mode off/audit, §6.2).
  - **Audit.** Team actions write `audit_log` rows with `team_id`, `object_kind`, `object_id`, `principal_id` and
    `auth='typed'` set:
    - `team_created`, `team_updated`;
    - `team_member_added`, `team_member_role_changed`, `team_member_removed`;
    - `principal_created`, `principal_updated`;
    - `project_created`.
- **`catalog/api.py`**:
  - `GET /projects?team=` (id or slug) lists one team's projects, and `GET /projects/{id}` carries `team`.
  - `POST /projects {name, description?, team_id?, by}`:
    - `team_id` can be an id or a slug;
    - an archived team gives 409;
    - with no `team_id`, the project goes into the caller's only active team; a caller in several teams gets 400
      `{message, teams}` (C §5.2);
    - the project is audited team-scoped.
- **`main.py`**: registration only. That is the router plus the two middlewares, after `evicted_guard`.

## Deviations and notes for dependants

- **Outside the P18 file list:**
  - `catalog/store.py`: `create_project` takes `team_id`. It is a 3-line change, so the project row is written in
    its team in one INSERT.
- **List rows for other routers are annotated by a middleware** (`team_rows`), not in their handlers:
  - why: those files belong to running lanes (`datasets_api.py`, `engine/api.py`, `registry/api.py`, and
    `main.py`'s `/api/datasets` handler);
  - cost: the JSON is re-encoded for those GET paths only, about 10 ms on `/datasets`;
  - not covered: `team` is not declared in their OpenAPI response models;
  - **P19** touches every router anyway. It should move `team` into the handlers, where `visible_teams()` will
    filter, and delete `LIST_ROWS` and `team_rows`.
- **`POST /projects` without `team_id` returns 400 for anyone in two or more teams**, as C §5.2 specifies.
  - Today's UI (`RunList.tsx` New project → `catalog.createProject(name, by)`) sends no `team_id`. It still works for
    the many people who are only in `tm_default`.
  - **U1** must send the current team from the team switcher.
- **Not built here.** These belong to later phases:
  - team creation needing an admin or `policy.self_service_teams`: P19, through `require()`;
  - `idp-groups`: T3;
  - `/teams/{t}/audit` and `/audit/verify`, with the hash chain: P19;
  - service tokens: T3;
  - team archive.
- **`tm_default` has owners only when `ENSEMBLE_ADMINS` was set at the P01 backfill.** The last-owner rule applies
  only once a team has an owner.
- `principals.is_admin` is re-synced from `ENSEMBLE_ADMINS` whenever a name is seen.
- **For P21:** shares refer to `teams.id`. The `team_or_404` helper (id or slug), `team_refs()` and
  `ensure_principal()` are there to reuse.
