## Verdict

**What holds:**
- **Storage and engines.** The core chain gives identical normalised output and shas on SQLite, Postgres and MinIO.
  The chain is: a multi-table upload, keys, relations, `_op` and upsert deltas, a join, a frame, a join run, and verify.
- **Queue.** The 10M edit job survives `kill -9`, both mid-write and after the commit. The attempt-keyed lease holds
  in both forced orders: one version, one audit row, no duplicate `version_no`.
- **Concurrency.** Two APIs and two workers on one root give no duplicate `version_no` and no reused `__row_id`.
- **Sampling.** The same seed gives the same `sample_sha256` across processes and across SQLite and Postgres.
- **Fan-out guard.** It catches a 1:n made by a rewrite or by a wider-key delta.
- **Window aggregates.** Recomputed in pandas, they are cell-for-cell correct: boundaries, NULL dates, NULL keys and a 30k-row skewed key.
- **Join pins.** They survive new input versions: parity ok, and a byte-equal batch.
- **Carry and compaction.** The many-version carry and the compaction above 16 parts are exact against an independent model.

**What breaks:**
1. **Online scoring of a join model skips the join (critical).**
2. **Silent value changes on the delta path.** Lossy numeric casts, ambiguous dates, and a delta that invalidates a declared key and drops a relation.
3. **Join specs that validate but compute nothing.** Date windows on non-date columns, and DATE = TIMESTAMP keys.
4. **A join frame that can be built from wrong bytes and then verifies.**
5. **A one-row delta is refused.**

## Notes for later phases

- **F-X3 (suggested):**
  - **C1:** refuse `predict` / `score` on a join model with a 400 that points to batches, or materialise the row's join from the pinned inputs.
  - **M1:** a check warning (needs acknowledgement) when a delta invalidates a confirmed key or drops a relation.
  - **M2 and M3:** lossless-cast checks on delta and declared types, and `date_ambiguous` on deltas.
  - **M4:** no row floor for `delta` / `append`.
  - **M5 and M6:** type checks in join validation: a window column must be DATE/TIMESTAMP; DATE and TIMESTAMP keys are compared as TIMESTAMP.
  - **M7:** refuse the same table name twice at join creation, or support it in batches.
  - **M8:** `frames.materialise` re-hashes its inputs, or reads them through `verified_read`, before recording a frame.
  - **The 500s in m1–m10.**
- **P24:** M9. The join cache of a deleted version must be purgeable. Frame verify should report a deleted input.
- **U2:** the join builder should show coverage 0.0 as an error state. `/predict` and `/score` should not be offered for join models until C1 is fixed.
- **P10b report:** its claim that "only a raw-device edit gets past `stat`" is too strong (m9).
